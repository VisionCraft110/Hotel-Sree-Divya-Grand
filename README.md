# 🍽️ Hotel Sree Divya Grand 
Premium Restaurant Ordering Platform

A professionally crafted restaurant website developed for **Hotel Sree Divya Grand, Kadthal**, designed to enhance customer engagement and simplify the food ordering experience. The platform combines elegant design, intuitive navigation, and seamless ordering functionality to provide a modern digital presence for the restaurant.

🌐 **Live Website:**  https://hotelsreedivyagrand.in/

---

## ✨ Project Overview

Hotel Sree Divya Grand is known for delivering quality food and exceptional hospitality. This website was built to extend that experience online by allowing customers to explore the menu, manage orders effortlessly, and connect directly with the restaurant through WhatsApp.

The project focuses on performance, usability, and a premium visual experience across all devices.
